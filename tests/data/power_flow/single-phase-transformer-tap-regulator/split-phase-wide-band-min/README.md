# Validation case: a single-phase tap regulator on a centre-tapped transformer with a wide band, `min_voltage_tap`

Asymmetric power flow from a 22 kV three-phase feeder through a `bc` → `ab_center_tapped` transformer (22 kV / 240-0-240 V, 50 kVA, clock 6, centre-tap electrode, from-side taps −4..4 × 2.5 %) with an `ab` service line and unbalanced leg loads. Regulator 401 keeps the mean of the two leg voltages (to true earth) in its band, each leg with the line drop compensation of the service line. The band is three tap steps wide, so taps −3, −2 and −1 are valid; strategy `min_voltage_tap` must end on tap −1, the valid tap with the lowest voltage.

## Generation

The input (`input.json`), the OpenDSS models (`test.dss` with fixed taps) and the expected output are generated by `tests/data/generators/reduced_phase_opendss_cases.py`, with OpenDSS (via `opendssdirect.py`) as the reference solver. The generator scans every tap position with fixed taps, chooses `u_set` and `u_band`, and asserts that the expected taps are the only valid ones (or, for a wide band, the lowest or highest valid voltage), with a margin of 10 % of the band. Do not edit the files by hand; change the case definition in the script and regenerate. The modelling choices and the command to run are described at the top of the script.
